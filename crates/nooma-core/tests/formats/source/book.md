# Winter on the cape

The lamp is lit at four in the afternoon in December. The keeper winds the clockwork that turns the lens every three hours, and writes the wind and the visibility into the log.[^log]

## The fog signal

When the fog comes in from the strait, the horn sounds twice a minute. Its compressor is fed with diesel from the tank behind the boathouse.

| Item       | Monthly amount |

# Летние ночи

Летом маяк зажигают поздно, после одиннадцати вечера. Смотритель чистит линзу от соли и проверяет фитили.

## Гости

- Рыбаки приносят свежую треску
- Почтовый катер приходит по вторникам
- Орнитологи считают гнездовья кайр

# Repairs

The copper roof of the lantern room was replaced in the spring of the second year, after a storm bent the weather vane.

[^log]: The log book is kept in the drawer under the chart table.
