# Unclogging a slow kitchen sink drain

A sink that drains slowly, gurgles, or leaves standing water after washing dishes usually has a clog building up in the trap or pipe below — a mix of grease, food scraps, and soap residue rather than anything wrong with the tap itself.

## Simple fixes to try first

Start with the least invasive option: pour a kettle of hot (not boiling, if you have PVC pipes) water down the drain. If that doesn't help, try half a cup of baking soda followed by half a cup of vinegar, let it fizz for fifteen minutes, then flush with hot water. A sink plunger also works well — fill the basin with a couple of inches of water first so it creates suction.

## If the clog is stubborn

Place a bucket under the U-shaped trap beneath the sink, unscrew the slip nuts by hand or with pliers, and remove the trap to clear out whatever is stuck inside — this is usually where hair and food debris collect. Clean it out, check the rubber washers for wear, and reattach everything snugly before running water to test. Avoid chemical drain cleaners if you can, since they can damage older pipes over repeated use.
