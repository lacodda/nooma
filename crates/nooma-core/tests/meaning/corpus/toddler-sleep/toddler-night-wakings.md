# Toddler keeps waking up at night

Unlike trouble falling asleep at bedtime, frequent waking in the middle of the night — sometimes several times — is a separate problem with its own set of usual causes: growth spurts, teething discomfort, or a sleep regression tied to a developmental leap.

## Common culprits by age

Around 18 months and again near age two, many toddlers go through sleep regressions linked to language development or new physical skills, even if nothing else in their routine changed. Teething pain, especially with molars, tends to cause waking around 2-3am rather than trouble at bedtime itself. Growth spurts can also trigger extra night hunger in younger toddlers.

## What tends to help

Avoid fully waking the child when you respond — keep lights dim and voices low, and resist the urge to pick them up immediately if a brief pause shows they might resettle on their own. If waking clusters around a specific time each night, note it for a few nights; a consistent pattern often points to something physical (teething, a full bladder) rather than an emotional need, and can guide what to check first.
