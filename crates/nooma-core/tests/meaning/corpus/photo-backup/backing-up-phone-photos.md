# Backing up phone photos automatically

Losing years of photos to a broken or stolen phone is avoidable with a backup that runs in the background, without you having to remember to do anything manually each time you take a picture.

## Setting up automatic cloud backup

Most phones have a built-in photo backup service tied to your account — enable it in settings and choose whether to back up over Wi-Fi only (recommended if you have a limited data plan) or also over mobile data. Set it to back up in the original quality if storage allows, since compressed versions lose detail you might want later for printing or editing.

## Keeping a second copy

Cloud backup alone is good but not bulletproof — account issues or sync bugs happen occasionally. Periodically connect your phone to a computer and copy the photo folder to an external drive, or use a desktop app that mirrors your cloud library locally. Before deleting photos from your phone to free up space, confirm the upload actually finished by opening the cloud app and checking that the same photos appear there, not just that the upload bar disappeared.
