# Electric kettle won't heat water

If the kettle clicks on but the light stays off and the water stays cold, or it shuts off almost immediately after switching on, the problem is usually electrical rather than anything to do with mineral buildup inside.

## Checking the basics first

Confirm the outlet itself works by plugging in another appliance. Check that the kettle is seated correctly on its base — the contacts between the kettle and the power base can get slightly misaligned or corroded over time, especially if the kettle has been knocked around. Wipe the base contacts gently with a dry cloth and try again.

## When it's the heating element or safety switch

If the outlet is fine and the base contacts look clean but the kettle still won't heat, the heating element itself may have failed, or the thermal safety cutoff (which is designed to trip if the kettle boils dry) may be stuck in the tripped position. Some kettles let this reset itself once fully cooled; if not, and the kettle is out of warranty, it's often cheaper to replace it than to repair the element.
