# Bike chain keeps skipping while pedaling

If the chain suddenly jumps or skips over a gear while you're pedaling hard, especially in one or two specific gears, the cause is usually not the chain itself but the rear derailleur being slightly out of adjustment, or a worn cassette that no longer matches the chain's pitch evenly.

## Checking the derailleur first

Shift into the gear where skipping happens most and look at the derailleur cage — if it's not sitting directly under the cog, the cable tension is likely off. Most derailleurs have a small barrel adjuster near the shifter or on the derailleur itself; turning it a quarter turn at a time while pedaling in a repair stand usually resolves minor skipping.

## When it's the cassette, not the cable

If adjusting tension doesn't help and the skipping happens across several gears, worn cassette teeth are the more likely cause — they develop a shark-fin shape that a newer chain can't grip properly. In that case, replacing just the chain usually makes the skipping worse rather than better, since a fresh chain won't mesh with worn teeth; the cassette needs replacing too, ideally together with the chain.
